# Harbour of Saltmere

- **Look & feel:** wet slate roofs, rope smell, lamps that never quite reach the water.
- **Who is here:** [Marrow](../../characters/marrow.md), netmenders, a ferryman who will not cross at night.
- **Hooks:** a fourth light was seen last night, where no tower stands. Related: [The Fog](../../lore/the_fog.md).
