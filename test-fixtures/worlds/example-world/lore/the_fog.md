# The Fog

The Fog arrives at dusk and leaves at dawn. It repeats the last thing said aloud on the quay.
Lighthouse keepers bargain with it: a kept secret for a cleared channel.
See [Harbour of Saltmere](../setting/places/saltmere_harbour.md) and [Marrow the keeper](../characters/marrow.md).
